The tables are in `results.md`, generated by `python -m evals.report --units` from the
files in `raw/`. This page says how to read them, what they do not show, and records one
disagreement between a judge and a deterministic check. Every number is a count over
repeated runs.

## Reading the tables

**S6 to S8.** S8, the heaviest noise, did not score lower than S6: 100% against 90%
binding pass rate. The two S6 failures are two turns in different replays (20 turns per
session in all), so the +10 points is within run-to-run variation, not evidence that noise
helps. What the curve shows is that the noise in S7 and S8 costs nothing measurable at
this sample size.

**Where binding fails.** S5 (65%) and S3 (78%) carry most failures. 22 of 31 failures are
missing required facts and 8 are turns that should have called a tool and called none.

**Retrieval.** 80 of 85 expected documents came back (94%). The five misses are all
SIMPLE_004 ("Webex support SLA"), which never retrieves `it_kb/webex_login_issue.md`: the
reranker keeps the licence-assignment article and the cleanup memo instead, both about
Webex but not about support.

**`needs_human`.** 31 of 295 session turns (11%). 17 of them are labels no tool returned:
on turns with no tool call, Nova Lite writes `[citation]`, `[constraint]` or
`[established facts]` after a fact the user stated, although the prompt names exactly
those as forbidden. 7 are uncited sentences, 2 are both, and 5 are the gate or a failed
lookup. The invented-label case is the largest single cause and is not fixed in this
task; nothing here was tuned against it.

**Heuristics.** Read the "Turns that flip" column with care. S5 turn 3 passed 2 of 5
baselines and 3 of 3 runs with any one heuristic off, in four of the five ablations: that
turn is unstable, and its flip says nothing about the heuristic. Discounting it:

- `needs_tools_override` (fires on 3% of turns) is the one that matters most: without
  it, S2 turn 3 fails 3 of 3 (answered from a stale checklist), S9 turn 1 fails 3 of 3
  (no lookup), and S9 turn 5 fails 2 of 3. Binding pass rate drops 6 points.
- `fuzzy_name_lookup` (3%): without it S7 turns 2 and 3 and S8 turn 2 fail every run,
  the noisy sessions where the user types "rrachel stein". Minus 4 points.
- `title_chunk_demotion` (13%, the most frequent): without it S8 turn 1 fails every run,
  the VPN-steps turn it was added for. Minus 3 points.
- `citation_brace_repair` (3%): without it S7 turn 4 fails 2 of 3. No change overall.
- `self_access_confirmation` never fires in the golden sessions: none has a first-person
  access request. It fires on all six Rachel runs (`rachel-*.json`). With it off
  (`raw/ablation-rachel-self_access_confirmation.json`, three untraced runs), the Rachel
  turn ends `completed` without requesting an approval or building an `AccessDecision`
  in 3 of 3 runs, and every `WF_WEBEX_001` must-include item fails.

**Rachel.** The citation fix raised expected documents cited from 12 of 15 to 14 of 15,
and runs whose next steps give a document's option (the cleanup memo's reclaiming of
inactive seats) from 0 of 3 to 3 of 3. Runs citing all five documents stayed at 2 of 3:
one run still left the access management policy uncited. Every rubric check passed in
all six runs.

**S4.** Before the paused-thread fix, turn 9 (the wrap-up after the "file it" turn)
resumed the pause and repeated turn 8's nine tool calls in 3 of 3 runs, so it failed its
binding facts. After it, turn 9 is a normal turn with no tool calls and passes 3 of 3.

## One disagreement between a judge and a deterministic check

Two turns from the baselines, one each way.

**The judge caught what the check could not: SIMPLE_005** ("Which SaaS contracts expire
in the next 30 days?", baseline replay 1, trace `d09eae6f2501a4aebc5a819be9d3e3db`).
The answer opens "Three SaaS contracts expire within the next 30 days from today
(2026-07-01):" and then lists Webex (19 days), Salesforce (45 days, "outside the 30-day
window") and Slack (70 days, "outside the 30-day window"), each with a label. The
citation check passed the turn: every list line carries a label a tool returned, and the
opening line ends with a colon, which `needs_citation()` treats as a heading and skips.
`citation_quality_v1` scored it 0.3: "The claim about three SaaS contracts expiring within
the next 30 days is unsupported. Only the Webex contract expiration is supported by a
label." The judge is right: the headline is false, and its own list says so. The binding
check did fail this turn, but for another reason (it searched documents and never read
the `contracts` and `software_subscriptions` tables); nothing deterministic caught the
wrong count. A count claim against a listed set is the kind of error a label check
cannot see.

**The check was stricter than the judge: S5 turn 1** (baseline replay 4, trace
`daf9f18f5180528de21476da709d5f62`). The answer cites the MFA reset steps
`[mfa_reset.md#First-line Troubleshooting]`, then ends with an instruction: "Tell Daniel
to request a video check with his manager or HR to verify his identity, then have IT
reset his MFA factor in Okta Helpdesk so he can re-enroll immediately." The citation
check found that last sentence uncited and the turn ended `needs_human`.
`citation_quality_v1` scored it 1.0 ("All claims are supported by labels or attributed to
the user"). Here the judge is the better reader: the instruction restates the cited
steps. The check counts an imperative as a fact claim. Changing `needs_citation()` to
skip instructions would fix this turn and could let an uncited instruction with a new
fact through; it is left as found and reported, not tuned.

## Cost

Langfuse prices the agent's generations from the published on-demand price of Nova 2
Lite for the `us.` profile, $0.30 per million input tokens and $2.50 per million output
(`scripts/register_model_prices.py`). On that price the five baseline replays cost about
$1.43 over 3.63 million tokens. This is an estimate from a published price; the AWS bill
is the source of truth. The judges used about 0.70 million Nova Pro tokens, which
Langfuse shows at $0 because no Nova Pro price is registered.
